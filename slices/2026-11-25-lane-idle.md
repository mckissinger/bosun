## Brief

Outcome: Lanes stop going idle unseen. No session stops for context, a lane waiting on a merge is woken through the hub, the hub keeps a background watcher on the run's PRs and lanes, and the idle measure can be rerun.

Done-conditions:
L1. `verified 2026-11-25 bosun-verifier (claude-opus-5-5, high then medium deltas), runs FAIL, FAIL, PASS WITH FOLLOW-UPS; HEAD 44a9fc3` `scripts/hub-watch.py` exists. With `--repo` and at least one `--pr` or `--lane NAME=WORKTREE`, it prints each PR's state and each lane's last transcript activity. With `--status` it exits 0 at once. Otherwise it exits 0 with one line per event on the first of:
- a watched PR merges or closes, or already has at start;
- its checks turn red;
- it starts to conflict;
- it stops merging, confirmed by a second look: open, not queued and auto-merge off, or green with auto-merge on but behind its base (reported at once when already so at start);
- a lane active at start goes quiet for `--stale-minutes`.

  With no change by `--max-minutes` it exits 0 with a heartbeat line. It exits 2 on bad arguments (including an interval under 15 seconds) and 1 after three gh failures in a row for one PR. With `--hub`, a new hub watcher for the same repo stops the previous hub watcher; a watcher without `--hub` is never stopped by another. It uses no GNU `timeout`.
